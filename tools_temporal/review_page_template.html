Temporal TEASER pilot dataset

Clean sign-language clips with synthetic hand occlusions pasted on them, for training a temporal module on TEASER. Clips with too much real hand-over-face occlusion were dropped using the SMPL-X fits; the rest were cut into clean segments, and every synthetic hand sits inside one, so each covered frame has a true unoccluded target. Two kinds of fakes: parametric (calibrated episodes sampled from the real statistics) and replay (the dynamics of real episodes replayed with a clean hand). Nothing has been trained on this yet.

How many frames are occluded

Occluded = the hands cover more than 20% of the mouth/nose/chin region (the RGB2SMPLX occlusion protocol's region and threshold, used for real and fake alike). "Training windows" is the share a 16-frame training window shows occluded when a fraction p of the windows comes from the variants.

Severity per clip

The classes of the multilingual occlusion dataset: none = no occluded frame; severe = peak coverage ≥ 0.60 and an episode ≥ 300 ms; moderate = the rest. A variant counts its real and fake occlusion together.

What is in it

Clean frames: frames inside clean segments (24+ frames, no hand over the mouth, nose or chin, at least 2 frames from any real occlusion). Validation and test signers never appear in training; their variants use held-out hands and held-out signers' dynamics.
